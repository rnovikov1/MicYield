MicYield detects an active microphone, not speech. A call or an app that keeps its mic open also triggers muting. Turn off Automatic audio muting during calls to hear other people.

Before you turn it on.

What gets muted, what keeps playing, and how your sound comes back.

What happens to music and videos?

Music and videos keep playing silently. MicYield mutes your Mac’s default audio outputs while the microphone is active; it does not control playback.

What if I change the volume myself?

Your manual sound changes take priority. MicYield restores only the mute or volume changes it made, so it will not overwrite your changes after dictation.

Does it record or upload my voice?

No. MicYield checks whether a microphone input is active without recording audio. The app makes no network requests and needs no account or browser extension.

Can I leave it on during a call?

An open microphone in a call triggers the same behavior as dictation. Automatic audio muting can silence the people you are speaking with. Turn it off in the menu to hear the call. An app that keeps its input open can keep muting active.

Which Macs are supported?

MicYield requires macOS 14.2 or later. Choose the Apple Silicon download for M1 or newer Macs, or Intel for an Intel Mac. You can find your chip in Apple menu → About This Mac.

Some outputs, including certain HDMI devices, have no software mute or volume control. Custom per-app routing to other devices is not changed.

What if audio stays muted?

Turn off Automatic audio muting or quit MicYield to restore its changes, then check the Mac sound output. Recovery after a forced termination may wait until the next launch. Very short microphone sessions may be missed.

Give your voice some space.

Download MicYield, move it to Applications, and start dictating. No account, browser extension, or subscription required.
