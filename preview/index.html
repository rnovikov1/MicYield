Up and running in a minute.

1. Move MicYield to Applications.

Open the ZIP, drag MicYield into Applications, and launch it. Its icon appears in your menu bar.

2. Turn on automatic muting.

Enable Automatic audio muting. MicYield will quiet the audio on your Mac’s default outputs whenever your microphone is active. Music and videos keep playing silently.

3. Start dictating.

When your microphone turns on, MicYield mutes your audio. After the mic is idle, it restores the sound changes it made. Enable Open at login to have it ready every day.

During calls, turn off Automatic audio muting to hear the other participants. Read the FAQ.
