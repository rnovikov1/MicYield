Up and running in a minute

1. Move MicYield to Applications

Open the DMG, drag MicYield into Applications, and launch it. Its icon appears in your menu bar.

2. Turn on automatic muting

Enable Mute audio on mic use. MicYield will quiet the audio on your Mac’s default outputs whenever your microphone is active. Music and videos keep playing silently.

3. Start dictating

When you start dictating, MicYield mutes your audio. When you finish, your sound comes back. Enable Open at login to have it ready every day.

During calls, turn off Mute audio on mic use to hear the other participants. Read the FAQ.
